# ZEUS MAGIC SHEETS - What Is This? (Simple Explanation)

## The Big Picture

**ZEUS MAGIC SHEETS** is like a digital workbook or journal that helps people practice coding problems. Think of it like:

- 📝 **A practice notebook** for students learning to code
- 🎮 **A video game** where solving problems = leveling up
- 📊 **A progress tracker** that shows how much you've improved

---

## What Does It Do? (Plain English)

### 1. You Get "Sheets" (Problem Lists)

A "sheet" is just a collection of coding problems to solve. This app has several ready-made sheets:

| Sheet Name           | What It's For                                         |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **ZEUS A2Z DSA**     | Learning programming from scratch                     |
| **ZEUS SDE Sheet**   | Preparing for software engineer jobs                  |
| **CP-31 (800-1900)** | Competitive coding practice at different skill levels |

### 2. You Track Your Progress

For each problem, you can say:

- ❌ "I haven't started yet" (Pending)
- 🔄 "I'm working on it" (In Progress)
- ✅ "I solved it!" (Completed)

The app automatically calculates:

- 📈 Your completion percentage
- �️ How many Easy/Medium/Hard problems you've done

### 3. It Remembers Everything

Everything you do gets saved:

- 🔎 Your completed problems
- 🔥 Your daily streak (consecutive days of practice)
- 📝 Your personal notes on problems
- ⭐ Problems you want to revisit

It saves to your browser, so when you come back, everything is still there!

### 4. You Can Organize

You can:

- Create your own topics and subtopics
- Reorder problems by dragging and dropping
- Search for specific problems
- Filter by difficulty or status

### 5. Activity Tracker

There's a cool graph (like GitHub's contribution graph) that shows:

- 📅 Which days you solved problems
- 🔥 Your current streak (consecutive days)
- 🏆 Your best streak ever

## What's the "Magical" Theme About?

It's just for fun! The designers chose a wizard/magic theme:

- 🪄 "Manuscripts" instead of problem sets
- 📜 "Scrolls" instead of topics
- 🔥 "Streaks" for daily practice
- ⚡ "Patronus Charm" for random question picker

It makes practicing coding feel more like playing a game!

---

## What's the Tech Stack? (For Curious Folk)

If they ask "How does it work?":

- **React** - The programming language/framework that builds the interface
- **Zustand** - Manages all the data and state
- **LocalStorage** - Saves your progress in the browser
- **Tailwind CSS** - Makes it look pretty
- **Three.js** - Creates the animated star background
- **Framer Motion** - Adds smooth animations
